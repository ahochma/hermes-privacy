Hermes

Hermes is a private, personal assistant used only by its owner and their household. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

Privacy Policy

Hermes connects to the owner's own Google account (Gmail, Google Calendar and Google Health data) only with the owner's consent, to answer the owner's own questions and send the owner reminders and summaries.

  • Data is accessed only for the owner and is stored on the owner's own private server.
  • Data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Data is not used to train AI models.
  • The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, after which stored data is deleted on request.

Hermes's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
